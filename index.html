Build something that’s still running when the week ends.

Job-seeking technologists ship tangible climate prototypes against urgent NYC environmental challenges — scoped for scale from day one, presented live to judges and hiring climate-tech companies.

Apply to build → See how it runs Applications close September 1

Most climate hackathons end with a slide. This one ends with something running.

No ideation theatre. Over 48 hours of build time, post-secondary technologists work in teams against real NYC environmental briefs and leave with a working prototype — not a concept.

Every build is designed for scalability and practicality from the first commit, then presented live to a room of judges and climate-tech companies who are actively hiring.
